## Shared and interactive content

The header/footer inventory is repeated in each route snapshot. Six home
states cover four product tabs and both comparison modes. FAQ answers,
expanded solution copy, form labels/options/validation, pricing, legal text,
support information, canonical/title/meta/schema are included even when hidden
behind a native disclosure. Existing shared CSS/JS and robots/sitemap are frozen
by SHA-256. Forms are tested with intercepted requests, never real submissions.
